# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not disclose suspected vulnerabilities in public issues, discussions,
or pull requests.

Use GitHub's **Report a vulnerability** feature in this repository's Security
tab to submit a private report. Include:
- the affected repository, component, version, or commit;
- a concise description and potential impact;
- reproducible steps or a proof of concept, if safe to provide; and
- any suggested mitigation.

If private vulnerability reporting is unavailable, contact the repository
owner through the contact method on the SiNAVM GitHub profile and request a
private channel. Do not include exploit details in a public message.

## Coordinated disclosure

Allow the maintainer a reasonable opportunity to investigate and address a
valid report before public disclosure. Please coordinate publication timing
with the maintainer. Do not access, modify, or exfiltrate data that is not
your own, and do not disrupt services while testing.

## Scope and safe harbor

This policy applies only to assets and materials that SiNAVM owns or is
authorized to maintain. It does not authorize testing third-party services,
upstream projects, infrastructure, or accounts. Good-faith research that
follows this policy is welcomed; this policy does not grant legal immunity
or override applicable law.

## Response

Reports will be reviewed as reasonably practicable. No fixed response,
resolution, or remediation deadline is promised. Please keep the report
confidential while a fix or mitigation is being prepared.
